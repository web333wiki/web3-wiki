#explain-card 

In cryptocurrency networks, a [[Node|node]] is any computer that connects to the blockchain network. [[Node|Nodes]] play a crucial role in validating transactions, maintaining the blockchain [[Ledger|ledger]], and ensuring the network's security and decentralization.

Types of Nodes:

- Full [[Node|Nodes]]: Store the entire history of the blockchain and independently verify every transaction.
- Light [[Node|Nodes]]: Download only a portion of the blockchain and rely on full nodes for transaction verification.
- Mining [[Node|Nodes]]: Validate and record new transactions on the blockchain through the mining process.
