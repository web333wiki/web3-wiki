## Overview

[[MetaMask]] is a popular non-custodial cryptocurrency [[Wallet|wallet]] and gateway to blockchain applications, primarily for the [[Ethereum]] ecosystem and EVM-compatible chains. It is available as a browser extension and a mobile application, allowing users to store, manage, and transact with cryptocurrencies and [[NFT|NFTs]], as well as interact with decentralized applications ([[DApp|dApps]]) directly from their browser or phone.

- **Problem Solved:** Provides a user-friendly interface for managing crypto assets and interacting with [[DApp|dApps]], abstracting away some of the complexities of direct blockchain interaction.
- **Value Proposition:** Wide adoption, ease of use, supports multiple [[EVM]] chains, direct [[DApp|dApp]] interaction, and integration with [[Hardware [[Wallet|wallet]]|hardware wallets]].
- **Official Website:** [https://metamask.io/](https://metamask.io/)
- **Developed By:** ConsenSys
- **Status:** Live and widely used.

## Key Features

- **Multi-Chain Support:** Supports [[Ethereum]] and a vast number of other EVM-compatible networks (e.g., [[Polygon]], [[BNB chain|BNB Chain]], [[Avalanche]], [[Optimism]], [[Arbitrum]], [[Linea]]).
- **Token Management:** Allows users to store, send, and receive ETH and various [[ERC-20]], [[ERC-721]] ([[NFT]]), and [[ERC-1155]] tokens.
- **[[DApp|dApp]] Browser:** Enables users to connect to and interact with decentralized applications directly from the [[Wallet|wallet]] interface.
- **Swaps:** Integrated token swap functionality, sourcing [[Liquidity|liquidity]] from various decentralized exchanges ([[DEX|DEXs]]).
- **Security:** Non-custodial (users control their [[Private key|private keys]]), supports [[Hardware [[Wallet|wallet]]|hardware wallet]] integration (e.g., [[Ledger]], Trezor) for enhanced security.
- **[[Gas]] Fee Customization:** Allows users to customize gas fees for their transactions.
- **Developer Friendly:** Provides [[API|APIs]] for [[DApp|dApp]] developers to integrate [[Wallet|wallet]] connectivity.

## Technology

- **Type:** Software [[Wallet|wallet]] (browser extension and mobile app).
- **Platform:** Primarily [[Ethereum]] and EVM-compatible chains.
- **Key Management:** Users manage their own [[Seed|seed]] phrases and [[Private key|private keys]].

## Ecosystem & Use Cases

- **Target Audience:** Cryptocurrency users (beginners to advanced), [[DApp|dApp]] users, developers.
- **How It's Used:** Storing and transacting crypto, buying/selling/managing [[NFT|NFTs]], interacting with [[DeFI|DeFi]] protocols, participating in [[DAO|DAOs]], accessing [[Web3]] games.
